# DEEPER — DEEPER_BIBLE

*Tone bible for anyone writing copy for this game.*

## The world

Something enormous was buried, running, a long time ago — and the town on top of it grew into
an industry of digging anyway. You inherit a rig the size of a shed and a hole in the ground.
Everything down there was somebody's machine, somebody's city, somebody's fault line. It does
not care about you. It will absolutely break — everything eventually breaks — and the fun is
deciding which parts break first and what you pocket from the rubble.

## Voice rules

1. **Declarative, dry, specific.** "It kept that promise." beats "time stood still."
   Numbers and proper nouns are funnier than adjectives ("Stopped at 7:42").
2. **No fantasy vocabulary.** No "ancient evil", no "legendary artifacts". They are fossils,
   urns, coins, bells, and machines. The wonder is in scale and neglect, not mysticism.
3. **The world is indifferent, never cruel.** Hazards are physics with a schedule, not malice.
   The magma is not "out to get you"; it is simply still hot after all these years.
4. **Money is a character.** The buyer at the scale weighs your haul without looking up.
   The economy is dry, honest, and slightly sad. Price tags tell stories.
5. **Machines have personalities by sound.** The auger coughs, the hammerhead argues, the
   needle hisses, the maw *swallows*. Tool names are blue-collar, not epic: Scrap Auger,
   Twin-Tooth, Hammerhead, Thermic Needle, Seismic Rig, Rotary Excavator, Resonator, THE MAW.
6. **Strata are chapters with a straight face.** "Somebody mined here before you. They left
   rails, lamps, and debts." "This isn't geology. A city is down here, and it was buried
   running." Write each band like a place a person remembers, not a biome.
7. **Failure is a transaction, not a punishment.** You lost some ore. The hole is still
   yours. Go again.

## Microcopy examples (canonical tone)

- Banner: **THE WORKS** — "Dig. Fill the hopper. Sell at the works. Go deeper."
- Toast: "LEFT CLICK to dig · A/D to drive · E at the works to sell"
- Cargo full: "CARGO FULL — sell at the works (E at base)"
- Blocked mark: "Blocked site marked on your map."
- Return: "Welcome back. The world is how you left it."

## What DEEPER is about

Quiet industrial wonder. Permanence — your holes stay holes. The long walk back through your
own tunnels past machinery you outgrew. And one moment, deep in soft old dirt with a machine
that should not be legal, when the ground simply *opens* — and you remember every swing it
took to get here.
